FAQ

Common questions

What makes BrainPulse different from agencies like FuseLab or Clay?

Most UX agencies focus on visual design and prototyping. We integrate AI governance (NIST AI RMF, EU AI Act), human factors engineering (MIL-STD-1472H), and WCAG 2.2 AAA accessibility at the systems level — not as afterthoughts. We also deliver production-ready frontend code in React, Angular, and Vue, and use LLMs to accelerate SysML modeling and compliance documentation.

What industries do you specialize in?

Healthcare, government/federal, AI & ML, defense, finance, fintech, biotech, manufacturing, SaaS, and cybersecurity. Our deepest experience is in safety-critical environments where human factors, regulatory compliance, and accessibility are non-negotiable — like clinical diagnostic tools, government case processing platforms, and AI governance dashboards.

Do you only do design, or also development?

Both. We deliver end-to-end: user research, UX strategy, wireframing, visual design, interactive prototyping, design systems, and production-ready frontend code in React, Angular, or Vue — with accessibility testing baked into CI/CD pipelines. We also provide backend API integration guidance for data-heavy dashboards and AI products.

What is your design process?

Our process follows a disciplined engineering approach: (1) Discover — stakeholder interviews, user research, accessibility audit, competitive analysis; (2) Define — information architecture, user flows, requirements traceability, design principles; (3) Design — wireframes, prototypes, visual design, WCAG-compliant components, usability validation; (4) Deliver — production code, design systems, developer handoff, and ongoing support. Every decision is traceable to user research, FMEA, or usability risk assessment.

How do you handle AI governance and compliance?

We implement NIST AI Risk Management Framework, EU AI Act classification and compliance design, ISO/IEC 42001 AI management systems, algorithmic impact assessments, model card interfaces, human-in-the-loop oversight UIs, and audit trail visualization. We also design AI monitoring dashboards for drift detection, TEVV reporting, and human-override controls.

What does WCAG 2.2 AAA compliance mean?

WCAG 2.2 AAA is the highest level of web accessibility compliance. It requires all text to have 7:1 contrast ratios (vs. 4.5:1 for AA), enhanced focus indicators, 44px minimum touch targets, reduced motion support, and comprehensive keyboard navigation. Our own website meets these standards — and we build every client project to the same level.
