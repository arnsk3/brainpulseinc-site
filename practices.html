PRACTICE 01

AI Governance & Trust

"Most agencies design pretty AI dashboards. We architect the governance that makes those dashboards trustworthy to regulators, executives, and operators."

What this practice does

The AI Governance & Trust practice is BrainPulse's flagship offering — and our most differentiated capability. We design and engineer the governance layer that sits between AI models and the humans who deploy, audit, and depend on them. This means model cards, algorithmic impact assessments, human-in-the-loop oversight UIs, TEVV reporting dashboards, audit trail visualizations, and AI Bill of Materials documentation — all built to align with NIST AI RMF, EU AI Act, ISO/IEC 42001, and emerging US federal requirements.

Unlike pure-play AI consultancies that focus on model development, we focus on the human-facing infrastructure that makes AI deployments defensible. When a regulator asks "show me how you validated this model," our work is what your team shows them. When a board member asks "what's our AI risk exposure," our dashboards answer the question.

PRACTICE 02

Accessibility & Inclusive Design

"Accessibility isn't a checklist at the end of a project. It's the architecture decision that everything else gets built on."

What this practice does

The Accessibility & Inclusive Design practice treats accessibility as systems engineering, not as a compliance afterthought. We don't run quick audits and hand over remediation lists — we build the organizational programs, design system foundations, automated testing infrastructure, and ongoing governance that make WCAG 2.2 AAA, Section 508, and ADA Title II compliance a permanent property of your products rather than a one-time achievement.

This is the practice that grew out of Senthil's enterprise accessibility leadership at Best Buy (where he built the enterprise inclusive design program from the ground up) and the Social Security Administration (where he deployed SSA11y — automated accessibility scanning — across 12 application modules). Our work is informed by direct experience training engineering teams of 60+ people, building component libraries used by 8+ product teams, and shipping accessible interfaces to user populations exceeding 70 million people.

PRACTICE 03

Human Factors & Safety Engineering

"This is where 'usability' stops being a preference and starts being a safety requirement. When operator errors have consequences, design becomes engineering."

What this practice does

The Human Factors & Safety Engineering practice applies established engineering standards to interfaces where errors carry real consequences — defense operator stations where misclicks can endanger lives, medical devices where workflow errors affect patient outcomes, autonomous systems where situational awareness determines mission success. This isn't UX design with safety language sprinkled on top. It's the disciplined application of MIL-STD-1472H, ISO 14971, IEC 62366, and DoDI 5000.95 to interactive systems.

Our methodology integrates cognitive task analysis, Failure Mode and Effects Analysis (FMEA), Use-Related Risk Analysis (URRA), and human-in-the-loop simulation testing. We don't ship designs based on what looks clean in Figma. We ship designs validated against operator workload measurements, error probability estimates, and human performance benchmarks. Every interaction has a documented basis in evidence — not opinion.

PRACTICE 04

UX Engineering & Emerging Tech

"We don't hand off Figma files and disappear. We ship the React, the design system, the documentation, and the team training that keeps the work alive."

What this practice does

The UX Engineering & Emerging Tech practice is where BrainPulse delivers production-ready code, not just visual designs. We build React, Angular, and Vue.js frontends with accessible component libraries documented in Storybook. We use LLM-accelerated SysML modeling to compress weeks of systems engineering work into hours. And we work at the leading edge of multimodal AI experiences, AR/VR/XR for safety training, and data visualization for complex regulated environments.

This practice exists because too many design agencies stop at the handoff. They deliver Figma files, write a few specs, and consider their job done — leaving engineering teams to interpret, often resulting in implementations that drift from the design intent and fail accessibility audits. We close that gap by shipping the actual code alongside the design — with accessibility tokens, automated testing, and documentation that survives the transition into your team's ongoing development cycle.
